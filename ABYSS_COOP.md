# Abyss / Deep Realm Co-op

## Current Direction

The normal world is primarily **single-player**.

The current preferred multiplayer idea is to isolate co-op inside a separate realm such as the **Abyss / Deep Realm**.

This protects the complexity of personal world histories.

---

## Why Separate the Multiplayer Space?

Different players may have completely incompatible worlds.

Example:

Player A:
- Kingdom destroyed
- World Tree alive

Player B:
- Kingdom survives
- World Tree burned

Synchronizing these worlds would be extremely complicated.

The Abyss can exist **outside normal history**, allowing players from different timelines to meet without merging their main worlds.

---

## Party Size

Current candidate:

**1–4 players**

The mode must remain playable solo.

No essential game content should require a healthy online population.

---

## Structure

Current idea:

- dungeon format
- around 10 floors/segments as a basic run structure
- escalating difficulty
- strong enemies and bosses
- build-testing environment
- optional multiplayer roles

Exact run length and structure are unresolved.

---

## Role Design

Support-oriented jobs may exist.

However, every job should remain basically solo-capable.

Example:

A healer can:

- heal allies in multiplayer
- convert healing magic into holy damage or self-buffs when solo

A tank can:

- protect allies in multiplayer
- convert aggro/guard mechanics into counter damage when solo

The game should not create jobs that become useless without teammates.

---

## Time and Lore

The Abyss being outside normal time can explain:

- why players from different histories can meet
- why dead bosses can reappear
- why causality-distorted enemies exist
- why unusual loot or challenges appear

This also creates a natural home for difficult experimental content.

---

## Networking

Networking technology is intentionally unresolved.

Potential implementation directions could include host-based co-op rather than a full persistent MMO infrastructure.

Multiplayer should be added only after the single-player core is proven fun.
