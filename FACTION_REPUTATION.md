# Faction Reputation and Titles

## Avoid a Universal Good / Evil Meter

The game should not declare the player globally "good" or "evil."

Different groups should judge the player according to their own interests and values.

A hero to one faction can be a monster to another.

---

## Two-Axis Reputation

Current proposal:

**Trust × Fear**

Each major faction can track both.

Examples:

### High Trust / Low Fear
The player may be treated as a beloved hero or ally.

### Low Trust / High Fear
The player may be treated as a dangerous threat.

### High Trust / High Fear
The player may be relied upon but also feared.

### Low Trust / Low Fear
The player may be unknown or irrelevant.

This produces more interesting social states than one positive/negative number.

---

## Faction Examples

Potential factions include:

- Kingdom
- Demon faction(s)
- Elven faction(s)
- Religious orders
- Adventurer Guild
- criminal groups
- regional powers

Each faction can interpret the same action differently.

---

## NPC-Level Relationships

Important NPCs may additionally have personal relationship states.

These do not need the same complexity as major faction reputation.

Possible factors:

- trust
- fear
- debt
- hatred
- affection
- ideological agreement

The exact model remains open.

---

## Titles

Titles should primarily affect **recognition and world reaction**, not function as simple +10% stat accessories.

Examples:

- Kingslayer
- Dragonslayer
- Savior of a City
- Heretic
- Oathbreaker
- World Tree Burner

A title can influence:

- dialogue
- NPC distance
- guards becoming hostile
- enemies fleeing
- hidden NPCs approaching
- boss introductions
- boss move selection
- faction access
- hidden quests
- hidden jobs

---

## Equipped Title vs Historical Fact

Removing a title from the visible UI should not erase history.

The game may distinguish:

### Historical Flags
What the player actually did.

### Presented Title
What identity the player openly displays.

This creates interesting deception and recognition possibilities.

Example:

The player has the hidden historical flag Kingslayer.

If they do not display the title, ordinary citizens may not know.

A spy, king, or magical boss might still recognize them.

---

## Boss Reactions

Titles can affect bosses beyond dialogue.

Example:

A dragon sees Dragonslayer.

Possible reaction:

- opens with a more cautious move
- avoids a vulnerable attack
- enters aggression earlier
- uses anti-hunter behavior

This makes titles part of systemic gameplay rather than purely cosmetic flavor.

---

## Reputation and Time Travel

Faction reputation is part of the physical/world timeline.

Rewinding history rewinds faction reputation to that time.

However, the player may remember how a faction reacted in a previous future.

This knowledge can guide a different approach.
