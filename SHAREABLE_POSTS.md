# Shareable Posts

These are intentionally short, standalone versions of the main concepts.

They can be copied, edited, translated, reposted, or used as prompts for discussion.

---

## 1. Time Leap

### English

**What if an RPG let you rewind history, but only your knowledge survived?**

Your level, items, stats and skill mastery return to what they were at that point in time.

But you still remember hidden jobs, secrets, failed futures, NPC deaths, and conditions you discovered.

So progression is partly about becoming stronger — and partly about understanding the world.

Full open design:
https://github.com/mero807/time-leap-arpg-game-design

### 日本語

**過去に戻れるRPG。ただし未来から持ち帰れるのは「知識」だけだったら？**

レベル、装備、ステータス、スキル熟練度はその時点まで巻き戻る。

でも、未来で知った隠し職、秘密、NPCの死、世界の結末は主人公だけが覚えている。

強さではなく「世界を知ったこと」そのものが進行になるゲーム。

## 2. Hidden Jobs

### English

**What if an RPG had hidden jobs that were discovered through bizarre combinations of systems instead of a visible class tree?**

A hidden job might require:

- a specific Job Lv
- strange stat allocation
- a curse
- a historical event
- a hidden area
- mastery of two unrelated skills

Some requirements initially appear only as ???

The goal is to make character progression feel like exploration.

### 日本語

**職業ツリーに全部表示されず、変な条件の組み合わせで隠し職を発見するRPGって面白くない？**

職業Lv、ステ振り、呪い、過去の歴史、隠しマップ、スキル熟練度。

普通なら関係なさそうな条件が重なって、新しい職業が見つかる。

## 3. Causality Distortion

### English

**What if changing history in a time-travel RPG did not cost "mana" — but instead made reality increasingly wrong?**

Rewrite too much history and you may create:

- enemies that should not exist
- people from incompatible timelines
- broken regions
- hidden bosses
- time-related secret classes

The penalty for time travel becomes more content.

### 日本語

**歴史改変の代償が「回数制限」じゃなく、世界そのものが壊れていくことだったら？**

因果歪曲が増えると、本来存在しない敵、矛盾した時代のNPC、隠しボス、時空系の職業が現れる。

時間改変のペナルティそのものをコンテンツにする。

## 4. Adaptive Bosses

### English

**What if a boss did not have one fixed combo script, but noticed how you play?**

If you always dodge right, heal at the same timing, stay far away, or punish the same recovery window, the boss gradually changes which existing attacks it prefers.

No input-reading. No impossible attacks.

**Unpredictable is allowed. Unreadable is not.**

### 日本語

**毎回同じ攻撃パターンを覚えるボスじゃなくて、プレイヤーの癖を見て攻撃選択を変えるボス。**

右回避が多い、回復のタイミングが同じ、遠距離に逃げる、同じ隙だけ殴る。

それを観測して、既存モーションの選択率を変えてくる。

## 5. Living World

### English

**What if an RPG had no mandatory main quest, but the world still kept moving?**

Kingdoms, religions, demons, elves and guilds all have their own event chains.

Ignore a war and it may end without you.

Return to the past and interfere, and the future can become completely different.

The player is special — but the world is not centered on them.

### 日本語

**メインストーリーを強制しないのに、世界の歴史だけは勝手に進むRPG。**

王国、宗教、魔族、エルフ、ギルド。

プレイヤーが無視しても事件は進行する。

気に入らない未来を見たら過去へ戻って介入できる。

## 6. Buildcraft

### English

**What if a build was not just class + equipment?**

Race × Job × Weapon × Skills × Stats × Status Effects × Titles × History.

A curse might be a downside for one job and a resource for another.

A title might change a boss's behavior instead of giving +10% damage.

The goal is for players to discover combinations even the designer did not explicitly plan.

### 日本語

**職業＋装備だけじゃなく、種族・職業・武器・スキル・ステータス・状態異常・称号・歴史までビルド要素になったら？**

呪いがデメリットではなく資源になる職。

称号でボスの動きが変わる。

開発者が想定していなかった組み合わせまで成立するゲーム。
