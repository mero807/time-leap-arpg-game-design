# Crafting System

## Scope

Crafting should support build experimentation without turning the project into a full survival-crafting game.

Current priorities:

- weapon purchasing
- blacksmithing
- material drops
- gathering
- custom weapon construction

Cooking is currently considered unnecessary.

Alchemy is desirable but optional if scope becomes too large.

---

## NPC Blacksmith

Ordinary players can use NPC blacksmiths.

NPC crafting may be recipe-limited.

Example:

- Iron Ore + Wood -> Iron Sword
- Monster Fang + Iron Sword -> Fang Blade

NPCs provide accessibility without exposing the deepest crafting system.

---

## Blacksmith as a Combat Job

A player may become a Blacksmith-type job.

This job should still be able to fight.

Possible progression:

- Blacksmith
- Magic Smith
- Master Smith

Names are placeholders.

---

## Advanced Player Crafting

Blacksmith jobs can access deeper crafting freedom.

Possible weapon structure:

- base weapon
- main material
- secondary material
- special material
- modifier

Example:

- Greatsword
- Black Iron
- Fire Dragon Scale
- Vampire Blood

Possible result:

- heavy dark-metal greatsword
- fire property
- bleeding or life-related effect

The exact procedural system is unresolved.

---

## Material Sources

Materials can come from:

- bosses
- normal enemies
- faction loot
- mining
- gathering
- hidden regions
- historical variants

A destroyed kingdom might provide materials that do not exist in the saved version of that kingdom.

This creates interesting time/history crafting conditions.

---

## Equipment Philosophy

Equipment matters, but should not replace job identity.

Most build identity should come from:

- job
- weapon behavior
- skills
- stats
- statuses
- history

Equipment can provide:

- numeric support
- unique effects
- rare transformative properties

Example unique effect:

> While Feared, dodge becomes a short teleport.

Transformative items are more interesting than endless tiny percentage upgrades.

- Can recipes remain known after rewinding? Likely yes as knowledge.
- How many equipment slots exist?
